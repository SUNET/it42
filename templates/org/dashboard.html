    How the call board works

    Open a card to work on its call, application or review panel. Attention brings together staff actions, outstanding responses and panels to monitor.

    Group cards by call or by the work involved. Open a card’s menu to save a private note. Pausing a call moves its card to the Paused tab until you resume it. Deadlines and reviews continue.

    Drag a decided application to Send to archive, or choose Archive application now from its menu. Other decisions move to Archived after one month.
