About IT42

What you can do

IT42 is one system used by several Swedish government research funders. You can use it to apply for funding, manage the grants you have been given, and help review other people’s applications.

Questions

Good to know

Who can use IT42?
Researchers, reviewers, organization staff and funders at Swedish government research agencies.
How do I log in?
With Sweden Connect, using BankID, Freja eID or another approved e-ID.
Does it cost anything?
No. IT42 is free for applicants, reviewers and organizations.
Can I apply to several funders?
Yes. One account works for every funder that uses IT42.
Can I have more than one role?
Yes. You can apply for one grant and review applications for another.
Who can see my applications?
Only you, your organization and the people handling the call. Nothing in review is public.
