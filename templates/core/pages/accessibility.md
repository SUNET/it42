We want everyone to be able to use IT42, whatever device or assistive technology they use. This statement covers the public site and the boards you see after you sign in.

## How accessible IT42 is

We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 at level AA. IT42 is {{fully / partly / not}} compliant with the Swedish law on accessibility to digital public services (lagen om tillgänglighet till digital offentlig service, DOS-lagen).

You should be able to:

- Use the whole site with only a keyboard.
- Use the site with a screen reader.
- Zoom in up to 200% without losing content.
- Change colours and text size in your browser.
- Understand content written in plain language.
