This page explains which cookies IT42 uses on it42.vr, the public site and the pages you see after you sign in.

## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website saves in your browser. They help the site remember you between visits, for example that you are signed in.

## Cookies we use

We only use cookies we need to run IT42. We never use cookies for advertising or to track you across other sites.

| Cookie | What it does | Expires |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **`sessionid`** | Keeps you signed in | When you close the browser |
| **`saml_session`** | Remembers your sign-in with Sweden Connect while it is in progress | When you close the browser |
| **`csrftoken`** | Protects the forms you send from forgery by other sites | 1 year |

## Your choices

All three cookies are needed for IT42 to work, so there is nothing to switch off. You can still block or delete cookies in your browser settings:

- Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies
- Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security
- Safari: Settings → Privacy

> If you block the session cookie, you can't sign in to IT42.
