## Why we use it

- To let you sign in and use IT42.
- To send your application to the funder you apply to.
- To show you where each application is.
- To keep IT42 secure and fix problems.

We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Who can see your data

- Your profile and documents are visible only to you.
- The funder sees the applications sent to its grants, and only those. Its staff and the reviewers it seats on a panel work on its own site.
- Your organization sees the applications that name it, to sign them off.
- Reviewer names and scores stay with the funder and are never shown on it42.vr.

it42.vr, each funder and the organization site have their own database. They exchange data only as messages about a specific application, so a funder never sees your profile or your applications to other funders.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- See the data we have about you.
- Correct data that is wrong. You can change your profile entries and documents at any time.
- Ask us to delete your data.
- Ask us to limit how we use your data.
- Get your data in a format you can take elsewhere.
- Object to how we use your data.

To use your rights, contact us at {{email address}}. We answer within one month.
